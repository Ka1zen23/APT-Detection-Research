## Activities completed

- Completed the controlled E5 provenance representation ablation across operation-only (E5-A), operations plus entity attributes (E5-B) and full directed provenance (E5-C).
- Consolidated the E2-D threshold sensitivity results for validation percentiles P95 through P100.
- Consolidated the tie-aware alert-prioritisation analysis for E2-A through E2-D.
- Reviewed per-tactic recall, seen/unseen description performance and representation conflicts.
- Began the read-only research dashboard under `web/` to make the artifacts reviewable by supervisors.

## Experiments executed

- **E5-A**: operation-only autoencoder, seed 42, 100 epochs.
- **E5-B**: operations and entity attributes, seed 42, 100 epochs.
- **E5-C**: full provenance, seed 42, 100 epochs.
- **E2-T**: analytical percentile sweep over the existing E2 predictions.
- **E2-R**: analytical alert-budget simulation over the existing E2 predictions.

## Findings

- Operation-only representations are highly ambiguous: all 70 original positives share a description with 8,393 negatively labelled events.
- Adding entity attributes eliminates the observed exact-description conflicts for original positives and raises AP from 0.003219 to 0.018724.
- Full provenance reaches AP 0.021743, approximately 16.1% above the entity-based representation, but is slightly worse on benign FPR, attack FPR, precision and F1 at P99.
- E2-D remains the leading anomaly ranker (ROC-AUC 0.995584, AP 0.109080) and recovers 46 of 70 original positives within the top 500 alerts.
- The P99.95 threshold reduces E2-D false positives from 8,628 to 2,339 but reduces contextual recall from 77.33% to 49.33%.

## Problems encountered

- The ablation changes text length, ordering and formatting alongside content, so it cannot isolate the causal effect of relationships alone.
- The full-provenance test set contains 283 descriptions above the 256-token encoder limit.
- Original positives remain concentrated in two ATT&CK tactics (69 credentialAccess, 1 defenceEvasion), limiting per-tactic conclusions.

## Solutions implemented

- Interpreted E5 as an overall representation comparison rather than a pure relationship ablation.
- Kept original and contextual label metrics separate in every report.
- Labelled the P99.95 operating point as retrospective and exploratory.
- Retained P99 as the primary experimental operating point.

## Decisions made

- E2-D is the leading observed model but is not designated for deployment.
- No threshold will be selected solely from attack-test performance.
- The next experiment is E7 multiple-seed reproducibility with seeds 42, 123 and 456.
- Current findings remain preliminary until multi-seed and independent-scenario evidence is available.

## Remaining questions

- Do the E2-D improvements persist across random seeds?
- Does full provenance retain its AP advantage under a matched text-length control?
- How much of the contextual-label recall gap is caused by annotation coverage rather than model error?
- Can temporal correlation reduce the false-positive volume without sacrificing original-positive recall?

## Next steps

1. Run E7 with seeds 42, 123 and 456.
2. Report mean, standard deviation and individual outcomes for ROC-AUC, AP, precision, recall and alert-budget recovery.
3. Design an independent attack-scenario validation protocol.
4. Investigate chunk-aware embeddings for descriptions above the token limit.
